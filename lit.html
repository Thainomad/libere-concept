About LIT

Smooth. Versatile. Addictive.

LIT is a refined extrait de parfum created for those who want to leave a lasting impression without trying too hard. Rose brings refinement, saffron adds warmth, and oud gives the fragrance smooth depth and character. Crafted at a 28% parfum concentration, LIT balances versatility, longevity and presence — refined enough for special occasions, yet effortless enough to become your everyday signature scent.
